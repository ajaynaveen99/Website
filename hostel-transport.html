Hostel — a second home

More than 100 students live on campus. For many, the hostel is what makes their education possible at all: their village has no school beyond a certain class, or the daily journey is too long, too costly or too unsafe.

Boarders live under supervised care with wardens on site, structured study hours, nutritious meals, medical arrangements and a CCTV-monitored campus. The routine is deliberate — many of our children come from homes with no quiet corner to study in.

Transport — reaching 40+ villages

For day scholars, school transport connects Vidyashilp to villages across the Haveri region, bringing children from farming and daily-wage families to a CBSE classroom and home again the same day.
