About the School

Vidyashilp International School was established in 2007 by Navachetana Foundation (R), Haveri, to bring quality, English-medium, CBSE education to the children of Haveri district — an opportunity that once required families to send their children to distant cities.

The school stands on Hangal Road, Haveri, and serves students from Nursery to Class 10 as a co-educational institution with hostel facilities for boarding students. Its name — Vidyashilp, "the sculpting of knowledge" — reflects the school's belief that every child is shaped with care, like a sculptor shapes stone.

The school aims to inspire Respect, Integrity, Spirit and Excellence in every student, helping each child unleash their true potential through transformative education.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Which board is the school affiliated to?

Vidyashilp International School is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), New Delhi — Affiliation No. 830741. The medium of instruction is English.

Which classes can my child join?

The school offers classes from Nursery through Class 10 and is co-educational. Admissions are typically open to all classes, subject to seat availability.

Is there a hostel facility?

Yes. The school offers supervised hostel accommodation, making it possible for children from towns and villages across Haveri district and beyond to study here. Hostel seats are limited, so early enquiry is recommended.

What are the fees?

The school is run by Navachetana Foundation for purpose, not profit, and fees are kept affordable for the region. Please contact the school office for the current fee structure for your child's class.

How do I apply?

Visit the school at Shrikantappa Badavane, Hangal Road, Haveri, or call the school office. You will be guided through the application form, interaction and admission formalities.
