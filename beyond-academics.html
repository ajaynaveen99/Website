Beyond Academics — ಪಠ್ಯೇತರ ಚಟುವಟಿಕೆ

Marks matter. Childhood matters more.

Co-curricular Activities

Where children discover what they are good at

Many of our students are the first in their family to attend a school with a playground, a music room or a stage. What happens in those spaces builds the confidence that a classroom alone cannot.

🏃

Sports & Gymnasium

Physical education, games and gymnasium facilities that keep young bodies as active as young minds — and teach how to win and lose well.

🎵

Music & Performing Arts

Dedicated music and activity rooms where children find a voice, an instrument and an audience.

🎨

Art & Craft

Creative work that gives every child a way to express what words do not yet reach.

🏆

Competitions & Events

Inter-house and inter-school competitions, annual day, sports day and cultural events through the year.

👧

Every Girl on the Field

True to the Foundation's women-first mission, girls are encouraged into sports, leadership roles and the stage, not only the classroom.

🤝

Community & Service

Activities that connect students to their villages and teach that education carries an obligation to give back.

Our Values

Respect · Integrity · Spirit · Excellence

These four words are not posters on a wall. They are practised daily in classrooms, corridors, the hostel and the playground.

🙏

Respect

For teachers, for each other, and for every family regardless of what they earn.

⚖️

Integrity

Honesty in work and in conduct, especially when no one is watching.

🔥

Spirit

The willingness to try, to fail publicly, and to try again.

⭐

Excellence

Doing ordinary things unusually well — the habit that outlasts school.
