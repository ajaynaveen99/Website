Academic Excellence — ಶೈಕ್ಷಣಿಕ ಶ್ರೇಷ್ಠತೆ

A rigorous CBSE education, delivered close to home, for children who have rarely had the choice.

A national curriculum, taught locally

Vidyashilp International School follows the CBSE curriculum (Affiliation No. 830741) in English medium, from Nursery to Class 10. For most of our families, this is the first generation to study in English and sit a national board examination.

With a teacher–section ratio of 1:15, class sizes stay small enough for teachers to know each child by name, notice when one falls behind, and act on it early. The school also employs a special educator and a counsellor.

How we teach

Concept-first teaching rather than rote learning, continuous assessment through the year rather than a single verdict in March, and remedial support for students who need more time — because a child who has never had a tutor at home cannot be left to catch up alone.

The Journey

From first step to first board exam

Nursery – KG

Foundation years

Play-based early learning that builds curiosity, language and social skills — and makes school a place a child wants to be.

Classes 1 – 5

Primary years

Strong fundamentals in English, mathematics, science and languages, with reading habits built in the library from the earliest classes.

Classes 6 – 8

Middle years

Hands-on science and computer laboratory work, growing responsibility, and wider exposure through activities and inter-school competitions.

Classes 9 – 10

Secondary years

Focused preparation for the CBSE board examinations, with mentoring and guidance on each student's path beyond Class 10.

Two details worth pausing on

A teacher–section ratio of 1:15 is what makes individual attention possible. In many rural schools a single teacher handles far more.

The school employs a special educator and a counsellor and wellness teacher — provision that is rare in a district school, and it means a child who learns differently, or who is struggling, has somewhere to go.
