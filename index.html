The Education Crisis

India's Constitution promises every citizen justice, liberty, equality and fraternity. For millions of children from low-income families, the education system has not yet delivered on that promise — and the gap is widest in rural districts like ours.

Who We Are

A School Built for Every Child

In Haveri, many children dream of studying in high-quality schools but cannot afford the cost of education.

Navachetana Foundation established Vidyashilp International School to bridge this gap by providing affordable, inclusive, and future-ready education for rural children.

Today, the school serves hundreds of students from farming families, daily wage workers, and underserved communities — while maintaining high academic standards and one of the largest financial aid programmes among private schools in the district.

Founder's Note

Every Child Deserves a Chance

When we started this journey, we had one simple belief: no child should miss quality education because of financial circumstances.

Over the years, we have met children with extraordinary potential whose dreams were limited only by opportunity. Vidyashilp was built to change that reality.

Today, every classroom reminds us that education is the greatest investment we can make in our communities. Every scholarship, every teacher, every laboratory, and every lesson brings us one step closer to a future where every child has an equal chance to succeed.

We invite you to become a part of this journey.

Mr. Nagendra V. Mali Founder President
