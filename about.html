Our Story — ನಮ್ಮ ಕಥೆ

Twenty-five years of standing with the rural poor of Karnataka.

The Beginning — 1999

Navachetana Foundation (NCF) was established in 1999 in Haveri, Karnataka, by Nagendra V. Mali. A science graduate with a diploma in journalism, Mr. Mali carried an early and enduring commitment to serving disadvantaged populations. His flair for serving the poor and promoting rural development inspired him to build an organization dedicated to giving rural communities a new spirit — Navachetana.

Growth of a Vision

Working closely with village communities, NCF organized rural women into self-help groups, promoted child education and school programs, and championed rural development across the Haveri region. Along the way came a powerful realization: quality education is the surest and most lasting way to lift a family out of poverty.

That conviction took concrete shape in 2007, when the Foundation established Vidyashilp International School on Hangal Road, Haveri — a CBSE-affiliated, English-medium, co-educational school serving children from Nursery to Class 10, with hostel facilities that bring quality education within reach of families across the district. Today the school stands as the Foundation's flagship institution and the living embodiment of its mission.

Milestones

Our Journey

1999

Navachetana Foundation established in Haveri

Nagendra V. Mali founds NCF to promote rural development and serve the poor, beginning with grassroots community work and self-help group promotion.

2000s

Deepening grassroots work

Self-help groups, women's empowerment, child education and school programs expand across the Haveri region.

2007

Vidyashilp International School established

The Foundation founds its flagship institution on Hangal Road, Haveri — bringing English-medium, CBSE education (Affiliation No. 830741) to the children of the district, from Nursery to Class 10.

2010s

The school grows into a trusted name

With its library, labs, music rooms, sports facilities and hostel, Vidyashilp becomes a full-fledged campus where children from Haveri and surrounding villages receive a well-rounded education.

Today

A continuing mission

The Foundation continues its community-focused work in education and women's empowerment, with Vidyashilp International School at the heart of its service to the families of Haveri.

Our Institution

Vidyashilp International School, Haveri

The Foundation's flagship institution — run by Navachetana Foundation (R), Haveri.

🏫

CBSE, English Medium

Affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education, New Delhi (Affiliation No. 830741), offering English-medium education from Nursery to Class 10 as a co-educational school.

🏠

Full Campus with Hostel

Located at Shrikantappa Badavane, Hangal Road, Haveri — with library, science and computer labs, music rooms, sports and gymnasium facilities, medical care, CCTV security and hostel accommodation for boarders.

Education with Purpose

Guided by the values of Respect, Integrity, Spirit and Excellence, the school helps every child unleash their true potential — education run for purpose, not profit.
