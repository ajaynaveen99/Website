BEFORE YOU SAVE A PHOTO — RESIZE IT
-----------------------------------
Photos straight from a phone or camera are usually 4000-5000 pixels wide
and 3-6 MB. That is far too heavy for a website. Many of our visitors are
parents in Haveri on mobile data, and a 5 MB image can take 30+ seconds
to load on a slow connection.

Resize before saving:
  - Width: 1600 pixels is plenty for a full-width photo
  - Format: JPG, quality 80
  - Target file size: under 300 KB

Free tools: squoosh.app (browser, nothing to install), or Paint / Photos
on Windows (Resize -> Custom -> 1600 px wide).

NAMING
------
Use lowercase, no spaces, describe the content:
  campus.jpg          founder.jpg        classroom.jpg
  science-lab.jpg     hostel.jpg         sports-day.jpg
  shg-meeting.jpg     library.jpg        annual-day.jpg
