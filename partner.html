CSR & Institutional Partnerships

Fund something measurable

We work with companies and institutions on defined, reportable commitments — not general contributions. Every partnership comes with utilisation reporting and, where you want it, a visit to see the work.

🎓

Scholarship Cohort

Fund a named group of students for a full academic year, with progress reporting on each child you support.

🔬

Infrastructure & Equipment

A computer laboratory, science equipment, library books, sports facilities or hostel amenities — capital gifts that serve every batch that follows.

🚌

Transport & Access

Support the transport that brings children from 40+ villages to a CBSE classroom every morning.

👩‍🏫

Teacher Development

Fund training for our 43 teachers — the highest-leverage investment in a school, because it reaches every student they will ever teach.

🏠

Hostel Support

Sponsor residential students for whom boarding is the only route to finishing school.

📊

Reporting Included

Utilisation certificates, photographs and impact reporting as required by your CSR governance. See our governance page →

Start a Fundraising Campaign

Raise support among the people you know

Mark a birthday, an anniversary, a wedding or a milestone by raising funds for a child's year at school. We will give you the story, the photographs and the numbers to share — and tell you exactly what your campaign paid for.

How it works

1. Tell us what you would like to fund — a scholarship, a library, a laboratory.

2. We send you a short campaign pack: the goal, the cost, photos and the wording you can share.

3. You share it with family, friends or colleagues.

4. We report back on what your campaign achieved.
